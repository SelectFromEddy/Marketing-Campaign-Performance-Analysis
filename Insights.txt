KEY FINDINGS

PROJECT:
Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis

BUSINESS QUESTION:

How should advertising budget be allocated across
platforms and campaign objectives to maximize profit
and improve marketing efficiency?

--------------------------------------------------
FINDING 1: OVERALL MARKETING PERFORMANCE
--------------------------------------------------

The company generated approximately $240.7M in
profit from $43.46M in advertising spend.

Marketing campaigns achieved an average ROAS of
7.52x, meaning each dollar spent on advertising
generated over $7 in revenue on average.

With more than 655K conversions generated, the
results suggest that the overall marketing strategy
was effective and produced strong business outcomes.

--------------------------------------------------
FINDING 2: PLATFORM PERFORMANCE
--------------------------------------------------

Google Ads was the highest-performing platform,
generating approximately $80M in profit and
contributing about 33% of total profit.

Google Ads and Facebook combined generated nearly
60% of all profit, making them the strongest
contributors to overall marketing performance.

These results suggest that both platforms play a
major role in driving profitability and should
remain key areas of focus for future investment.

--------------------------------------------------
FINDING 3: CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE PERFORMANCE
--------------------------------------------------

Conversion campaigns achieved the highest average
ROAS at 9.20x.

Compared to Brand Awareness campaigns, Conversion
campaigns produced approximately 2.3 times greater
return on advertising spend.

This suggests that campaigns focused on conversions
deliver the strongest financial returns and may
offer the best opportunity for future budget growth.

--------------------------------------------------
FINDING 4: CUSTOMER ACQUISITION EFFICIENCY
--------------------------------------------------

Facebook achieved the lowest average CPA at
approximately $118 per acquisition while also
generating the second-highest profit at $64M.

In comparison, LinkedIn produced the highest CPA
at approximately $379 per acquisition while
generating only $23M in profit.

These results suggest that Facebook provides a
strong balance between profitability and customer
acquisition efficiency.

--------------------------------------------------
FINDING 5: BUDGET ALLOCATION OPPORTUNITY
--------------------------------------------------

The analysis identified three key strengths:

- Google Ads generated the highest total profit.
- Conversion campaigns produced the strongest ROAS.
- Facebook achieved the lowest CPA.

Together, these findings indicate that future
budget allocation decisions should prioritize
high-profit platforms and campaign objectives while
continuing to monitor acquisition efficiency.

--------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDATION
--------------------------------------------------

Based on the analysis, future advertising budgets
should prioritize Google Ads and Conversion-focused
campaigns because they consistently produced the
strongest financial results.

Facebook should continue to be used as a cost-
efficient customer acquisition channel due to its
combination of strong profitability and low CPA.

Lower-performing platforms and campaign objectives
should be reviewed for optimization opportunities
before receiving additional budget.

Overall, marketing investments should focus on
initiatives that demonstrate strong profitability,
high ROAS, and efficient customer acquisition costs.
